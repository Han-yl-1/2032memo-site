The Three-Tier Frequency Ladder: From the Hubble Sphere to Regular Tetrahedral Knots

The foundation of the model pending test is minimal: the universe consists solely of distinct structures of the ether and the motion of the ether. Everything humanity calls distinct forms of matter, down to carbon-based humans themselves, reduces to this. The elementary ether structure is a regular tetrahedron, and all other material structures are formed by regular tetrahedra knotting together.

Wrapped around every ether structure is a region of ether space circulating back through the knot. The elementary ether is the smallest unit, at a frequency of roughly 4.3× 10102 Hz—1079 times the proton frequency—penetrating every structure: it pierces into the knot along the thumb axis and spirals outward counterclockwise in the plane of the four fingers at the speed of light by the right-hand rule, shaped like the field lines of a magnetic dipole. A knot is the persistent form of a vortex within the flow; the ether is neither created nor destroyed, satisfying continuity automatically; every knot advances together with the background flow at the same acceleration, establishing the equivalence principle whereby different materials fall at the same rate in a gravitational field. The knot's frequency f = mc2/h = c/λC corresponds to the number of times per second light-speed ether traverses that knot's own Compton wavelength.

On March 3, 2026, Zhang Xiangqian wrote down the normalization equation treating the entire Hubble sphere as a single de Broglie wave packet: 4π2 r3 c2 / (T2 G h ν) = 1. Taking r = c/H and T = 2π/H by Kepler's third law, combined with Mc2 = hν, yields ν = c5/(hGH) = 2πνether = 2.725× 10103 Hz. On this basis, Lu Qianhe and Zhang Xiangqian pre-registered the Law of the Intermediate Scale—the Planck frequency is the geometric mean of the cosmic expansion rate H and the ether frequency νether, and at every epoch t:

νether(t) · H(t) = νP2 = 8.715× 1084 Hz2,      ℏ · G = (c5) / (4π2 · νether(t) · H(t))

This equality erects a three-tier frequency ladder. The top tier is the macroscopic cosmic layer: at a central Hubble constant of 62.0 km·s-1·Mpc-1, the expansion rate H is 2.009× 10-18 s-1, with the pre-registered interval 61.8–63.5 corresponding to 2.003× 10-18–2.058× 10-18 s-1. The middle tier sits at humanity's observational boundary: the Planck frequency νP = √(c5/ℏ G)/2π = 2.952× 1042 Hz, corresponding to a Planck mass of 21.76 micrograms; the geometric midpoint frequency of the lower span is √(HνP) = 2.435 THz, interval 2.43–2.46 THz. The bottom tier is the microscopic ether background: the ether frequency νether = νP2/H = 4.337× 10102 Hz, interval 4.235× 10102–4.351× 10102 Hz. The span ratio per tier is N = νP/H = 1.469× 1060, interval 1.435× 1060–1.474× 1060.

Cosmic evolution divides into two stages. The early non-uniform stage runs from the birth of the universe to an age of 3.912 billion years (interval 3.641–3.950 billion years), marked by violent collisions, mergers, and knotting among high-temperature radiation and smaller-unit material structures; within it, νether = 1096 Hz and 1.51× 1096 Hz correspond to cosmic ages of 1,866 years and 2,833 years, respectively. The transition occurred 9.900 billion years ago, interval 9.893–9.933 billion years ago, at redshift zs = 1.686 (interval 1.669–1.817; the acoustic-angle benchmark zs = 1.721 corresponds to an age of 3.837 billion years and scale ratio 1+zs = 2.721 ≈ e). Large-scale galactic structure stabilized and took shape, ether field pressure of ten orders of magnitude per dimension across twelve-dimensional phase space canceled out into an N-2≈ 2.2× 10-120 stochastic steady state, and the expansion rate shifted into uniform coasting H(z) = H0(1+z). Over the ensuing 9.900 billion years, the ether frequency grew linearly with cosmic age from 1.615× 10102 Hz to today's 4.337× 10102 Hz while the expansion rate fell in inverse proportion, their product locked at 8.715× 1084 Hz2—ensuring that ℏ and G do not drift over time and satisfying the lunar laser ranging bound |Ġ/G| < 1.5× 10-13/yr. Added together, the two stages come to 13.812 billion years (rounded in the pre-registration table at H0 t0 = 0.876 to a central current cosmic age of 13.82 billion years).
