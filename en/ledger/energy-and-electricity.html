Section 54 · Primary Energy: How Much Is Burned Every Second

What Counts as Primary Energy

Primary energy is booked in joules. In 2025 it exceeded 600 exajoules; divided by the seconds in a year it is 19 terawatts, and divided again by 8.162 billion people, 2,330 watts — the power the civilization continuously sustains for one person.

Primary energy: energy obtained directly from nature without conversion (coal, oil, gas, uranium, hydro, wind, solar, biomass).

Three historic milestones of 2025 (Ember 2026, A):

  1. Renewable generation was 33.8%, exceeding coal-fired generation (33.0%) for the first time — the first time in more than a hundred years.
  2. Solar generation grew 30% (+636 TWh), exceeding wind for the first time, and approaching nuclear.
  3. Wind and solar together covered 99% of the growth in global electricity demand in 2025, with fossil generation roughly flat.

Fossil (coal + gas + oil) still generates about 57.4% of electricity.
